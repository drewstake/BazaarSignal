# Signed-in night market artwork

Created from scratch with the built-in imagegen tool. No landing-page image was used as an input. Saved as `workspace-courtyard.png`.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background artwork for the signed-in BazaarSignal portfolio web application.
Create a BRAND NEW wide 3:2 high-resolution night market scene, unrelated in composition to a treasure-chest hero landing page. A Minecraft / SkyBlock inspired voxel merchant courtyard on a floating island at night, viewed from a quiet stone trading balcony. Deep ink navy sky, distant tiny floating islands, warm amber hanging lanterns, subtle mint and cyan magical lighting. The architecture is arranged around the far LEFT and RIGHT edges: slim stone parapets, small wooden market canopies, hanging greenery, a few crates and cobblestone pavement near the bottom corners. A distant lantern-lined bridge and market rooftops create depth behind the open center.
Composition: crucially, the middle 75% of the image is an unobtrusive dark navy atmospheric open area with low contrast, suitable for readable real UI panels to be layered over it. Decorative environment stays along the sides and bottom, with a narrow skyline at the top. No huge centered object or focal illustration, no chest with floating items, no circular moon near foreground, no portfolio panel. Finely detailed pixel/voxel material textures, cinematic soft night illumination, pleasing amber-vs-emerald highlights, professional gaming interface backdrop. Completely different scene and framing from the landing page. No text, no logos, no UI, no charts, no buttons, no interface frames. Fill entire canvas with artwork; no outer border.
