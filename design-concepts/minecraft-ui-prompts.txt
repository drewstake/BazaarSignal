BASE PROMPT

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one highly creative, polished desktop web-app UI concept image for BazaarSignal, a Minecraft Hypixel SkyBlock companion for discovering bazaar flips and auction bargains.
Audience: kids and teens who actually play Minecraft, plus longtime players. Make it playful, clever, adventurous, and cool without being babyish. The interface should feel native to Minecraft culture: collectible pixel-art items, inventory slots, rarity colors, block-built geometry, tactile game-menu controls, and a little environmental storytelling.
This is a usable website screen, viewed straight on, filling a wide 16:10 landscape image. No laptop, room, browser chrome, perspective mockup, or collage. The interface occupies at least 85% of the image. Keep ornamental scenery at the margins. Clear hierarchy, generous spacing, legible labels, and easy-to-scan prices. Pixel/bitmap lettering for brand and short headings; clean readable type for controls, item names, and numbers.
Show the brand exactly "BazaarSignal", navigation "Bazaar", "Auctions", "My Flips", "Watchlist", a clear item search box, and an obvious active navigation state. Build the main experience around finding and inspecting interesting items. Include detailed recognizable voxel/pixel item art such as a glowing enchanted sword, a purple Summoning Eye, an enchanted diamond block, and a golden cookie. Items must be real illustrations with texture and silhouette, not generic line icons.
Use 4–6 attractive item opportunities with short readable labels, buy and resale estimates in coins, positive profit, rarity, and a clear action. Feature one selected opportunity in a compact deal inspector with a buy-to-sell comparison, expected profit, and an "Add to watchlist" button. Integrate a small "Sample prices" label. Use plausible illustrative numbers, not claims about live prices.
Art direct a distinctive layout, not the conventional four KPI cards plus line chart. Avoid corporate finance dashboards, generic SaaS cards, glassmorphism, bland pill controls, stock illustrations, photorealism, baby mascots, gambling motifs, loot boxes, and large hero artwork that overwhelms the functional UI. No code, annotations, design-board labels, or watermark.

CRAFTING DESK

Design direction: THE CRAFTING DESK. Reimagine Minecraft's inventory/crafting interface as a brilliant modern web tool. Near-black deepslate, soft stone-gray slab panels, parchment-white type, emerald and lime highlights, and small gold coin icons. Bold crisp stepped panel corners with thin beveled pixel edges, carefully restrained texture. The main content is a wide curated inventory grid of unusually beautiful pixel-art items, with a selected slot that connects visually to a compact right-side flip recipe: buy item → sell item → profit. A slim left utility rail with meaningful pixel icons and text, a horizontal category strip that resembles hotbar tabs, and an orange NEW tag on the best listing. Heading: "Find your next flip." A few tiny grass tufts and torchlight at the outer margins add Minecraft character. Confident, crisp, tactile and gamer-cool, like an indie game UI art director designed a real trading companion.

SKY ISLAND MARKET

Design direction: SKY ISLAND MARKET. A bright, adventurous market stall in the SkyBlock universe translated into an exceptional website interface. Cream parchment working surfaces, sky blue, leafy green, warm oak brown, and golden coin highlights. A narrow illustrated skyline of floating grassy voxel islands with tiny merchant awnings runs behind the header, with ample clean space for all working UI below. Main layout is an asymmetric market board: a prominent DEAL OF THE DAY item showcase on the left, a tidy two-row grid of shorter item listing cards in the middle, a narrow pinned-paper price comparison on the right. Header navigation feels like labeled tabs hanging from a crafted wooden market sign, refined and lightweight rather than heavy skeuomorphism. Heading: "Good loot. Better deals." Real readable UI, clean typography, small pixel details, collectible item rarity ribbons, appealing little illustrated chest for saved items. Cheerful and charming but sophisticated enough for a teenage player's daily tool. Avoid making the page mostly scenery or an RPG screenshot.

ENDER EXCHANGE

Design direction: ENDER EXCHANGE. An imaginative late-night treasure hunting terminal built from obsidian and enchanted inventory panels. Near-black violet background, rich indigo surfaces, electric lavender selections, crisp teal positive profits, occasional legendary gold. Small pixel particles and restrained enchantment glints at panel edges, not ubiquitous bloom. Distinctive asymmetric composition: horizontal navigation at top, a compact row of market category chips, a large feature auction card with a vivid floating enchanted sword at left; dense but playful small deal rows underneath; an anchored inventory-like inspector on the right with selected item, resale range, and clear net profit. Heading: "Rare finds. Real potential." The featured item should be impressive, dimensional voxel art without drowning out the product. Chunky custom geometric framing, slightly offset inventory panels, charming rarity indicators, blocky buttons with visible physical depth. Cool, mysterious and energetic Minecraft fan-site design, not cyberpunk, crypto trading, generic esports branding, or a flat SaaS dashboard.
