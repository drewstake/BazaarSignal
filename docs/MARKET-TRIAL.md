## Scheduling and trial boundaries

[Cloud Scheduler has minute resolution](https://docs.cloud.google.com/scheduler/docs/configuring/cron-job-schedules). Reusing the existing job gives at most **60 opportunities/hour**, below the requested 120. There is no in-function loop or sleep to create the missing half-minute invocation. Upstream cadence, 43-page historical auction snapshots, retries and budget limits can reduce completed refreshes further. A successful unchanged-generation probe is explicitly not counted as a full refresh.

`collector/trial.ts` stores a shared CAS ledger with an absolute deadline of at most 15 minutes, stable scheduled-event identities, no refunded reservations, bounded invocation envelopes, and a stop latch. `collector/trial-runtime.ts` connects it to both real Functions handlers, the collector, cache reads and responses. `collector/trial-google.ts` issues explicitly counted REST operations without hidden SDK retries; each listing page is a separate Class A attempt. It bounds incoming data and counts compressed uploads/downloads separately from conservative transfer reservations. Redirects cannot silently create another upstream request. Exhausting an invocation's reservation cancels its parallel I/O and latches optional work closed.

`collector/trial-preflight.ts` validates complete fresh evidence and the entire trial's pessimistic consumption plus overhead. It rejects required unknown meters, missing shutdown/deployment/scope/transfer verification and trials spanning an allowance reset. The only alternative to a measured or conservatively bounded baseline is verified zero additional consumption of an allowlisted discrete operation, as detailed below. `collector/trial-launch.ts` now installs the ledger before activation, claims each trial identity once with shared CAS, refuses replacement of an active/draining trial, and attempts both infrastructure containment and a durable ledger stop after failed or ambiguous activation. Concurrent losing launchers cannot stop the winning launch. No deadline is extended by launch or retry.

`collector/trial-launch-google.ts` checks actual deployed immutable images, ready revisions and traffic, service accounts, request-based billing, resource/concurrency limits, matching trial ID/deadlines, the minute schedule and private invocation policies. Read-only inspection confirmed that Cloud Run reports full ready-revision paths but short traffic revision names, and retains a mutable image tag in the Service template. The launcher normalizes the names and reads the serving Revision's resolved digest. It rejects public inherited project access and unverified organization/folder inheritance, and requests IAM policy version 3 to preserve conditional bindings and their etag. Shutdown permission evidence and complete allowance evidence still require operator verification; a successful local test is not that evidence. The deployment guard remains closed because pre-deployment cost bounds and live accounting are incomplete.

The last scheduled minute is reserved for shutdown, so this implementation may stop at approximately 14 minutes. The existing scheduled invocation pauses the job and removes public API invokers; expired/missing configuration or accounting takes the same path. Both containment actions are attempted even if one fails, and their resulting state is checked. Control-plane failure is reported as incomplete shutdown, never successful containment. All optional work still checks the deadline after process restart. An outage delaying Scheduler delivery or denying IAM changes can delay infrastructure containment; this is not a billing cap.

Production browser activation additionally requires `VITE_MARKET_UPDATES_PAUSED=false`, a valid `VITE_MARKET_TRIAL_ID`, and a valid `VITE_MARKET_TRIAL_END` ISO deadline within 15 minutes. Functions also require the matching `MARKET_TRIAL_ID`, `MARKET_TRIAL_START` and `MARKET_TRIAL_END`; a different ledger identity is refused even when its dates match. The browser deadline timer aborts in-flight polling without requiring another response; cached or late responses cannot extend it. Old-trial cached data remains reusable with its original data timestamp, but its expired grant cannot pause a separately authorized new trial or reactivate an expired one. Reloading after expiry stays paused. Cross-tab locks/cache reuse remain in place. Apps Script requires bounded `MARKET_TRIAL_START` and `MARKET_TRIAL_END` Script Properties as well as the explicit unpaused flag, otherwise market calls stop while account/target/queued-email paths remain available.

## Interpretation and provisional rate guidance

120 full collections/hour with four uploads each cannot fit 5,000 monthly Class A operations: its maximum is over 71 times that allowance before other activity. Even 56 one-page cleanups/day uses 34.72% by itself. The implementation separates opportunities from actual work, but no sustainable continuous rate has yet been established by this trial. Keep production paused until the launch gates and measurements are complete.

For comparison only, the earlier proposed hourly collection with at most four uploads and one daily listing is 3,007 Class A operations over 31 days (60.14%). It is an **estimate**, excludes other storage activity, and cannot by itself approve continuous operation under all other limits. Hourly data would become stale under the existing three-minute comparison protection; fresh comparisons and price-based alerts would only be available briefly after each successful update. Do not relax that protection to make a slow schedule appear fresh.

Startup/admission, control-plane delays, denied/retried external traffic, bytes already transferred, monitoring lag, builds, retained versions/images and other account activity can incur cost before application checks stop work. This implementation reduces risk; it does not technically prohibit paid overage. No new charge amount has been verified, and prior accrued charges remain separate from the trial.
